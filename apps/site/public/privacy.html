Privacy

Last updated 13 October 2026

What we collect here

When you sign up, we store the email address you typed, which of the page’s forms you used, and the time. That is all. This site has no analytics, no tracking cookies and no ad pixels.

Why

To email you about your free CV and early access to resumeapplyr. We don’t sell your address or share it with anyone else.

Where it is kept

In a database at Cloudflare, which hosts this site. Like any web host, Cloudflare handles your IP address to serve you the page. The fonts come from Google Fonts, which also sees your IP address when it sends them.

How long

Until you ask us to delete it, or until we close the waiting list, whichever comes first.

Seeing or deleting your data

Reply to any email from us and ask. We will tell you what we hold, or delete it, within 30 days.

The desktop app

  • The resumeapplyr app keeps your profile, CVs and applications on your own computer. It does not send them to us.
  • Its AI work runs through your own Claude Code or Codex account, under that provider’s terms.
  • Job boards and employers receive what you choose to send them when you apply.
